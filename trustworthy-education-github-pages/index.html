01 / Overview

Educational AI needs trust at every step.

Sensitive learner data and consequential decisions make trustworthiness central to intelligent education.

Research on trustworthy educational AI is growing, yet remains scattered across tasks and technical concerns. This survey connects the educational task landscape with a structured account of trustworthiness methods, limitations, and future directions.

03 / Trustworthiness

Five perspectives on trustworthy systems.

Explore the methodological families and the challenges that remain.

01

Safety & privacy

Protect sensitive learning data.

Method families

Federated learning · Machine unlearning · Privacy attacks

Open challenge

Privacy protection must account for leakage risks and practical deployment constraints.

02

Robustness

Keep predictions reliable under imperfect observations.

Method families

Denoising · Anomaly-aware modeling

Open challenge

Distinguishing noise from meaningful variation remains difficult across learners and platforms.

03

Fairness

Reduce inequity in educational decisions.

Method families

Debiasing · Fairness-aware training

Open challenge

Long-term effects and incomplete sensitive attributes complicate fair learner modeling.

04

Explainability

Make educational decisions understandable.

Method families

Intrinsic models · Post-hoc attribution · LLM explanations

Open challenge

Explanations need fidelity and pedagogical reliability, beyond fluent descriptions.

05

Sustainability

Support efficient and lasting deployment.

Method families

Model compression · Acceleration

Open challenge

Efficiency must be considered alongside adaptability and long-term maintenance.

04 / Future directions

Toward trust across the whole learning process.

A research agenda connecting practical challenges with emerging educational AI.

Challenge-driven

Beyond isolated decisions

  • Trust across interconnected educational pipelines.
  • Long-term and sequential trust modeling.
  • Realistic noise, missingness, and distribution shifts.
Emerging frontiers

New capabilities, new questions

  • Trustworthy multimodal educational modeling.
  • Reliable and aligned LLM-enabled education.
